# Lesson 1 — The Glimmerwort Body

Glimmerworts are fictional organisms that live in the dark ice caverns of Veyra, an imaginary moon. They never move from the spot where they first take root.

A glimmerwort's body has three parts:

- The **crown** is the top of the body. It contains the lumeplasts, the organs that make light.
- The **stalk** connects the crown to the bottom of the body. Tubes inside the stalk, called veskways, carry fluid and dissolved minerals up and down.
- The **root-mat** is a flat web at the bottom that grips the ice and absorbs a mineral called selite.

Every glimmerwort cell is wrapped in a hard outer layer made of braided silica threads, called the vesk. The vesk protects the cell from the cold.

Each cell also contains one core-bead, a small sphere that stores the glimmerwort's hereditary code. The code is written with four kinds of units called quadrons, named N, O, R and T. When a cell divides, the core-bead is copied so both new cells get one.

Glimmerworts need selite to build their vesk. A glimmerwort that cannot get enough selite grows a thin, brittle vesk and its cells crack in the cold.
