# Lesson 3 — Reproduction and Signals

## Triple budding

A glimmerwort becomes mature at an age of 40 cycles (a cycle is one turn of Veyra around its planet). A mature glimmerwort reproduces by triple budding: it grows exactly three buds at the same time, one on each side of its stalk. The buds stay attached for 9 cycles. Then they break off, drift a short distance and take root. Each bud receives a copy of the parent's core-bead, so buds are genetically identical to their parent.

## Glow signals

Glimmerworts communicate with the color of their glow:

- a **blue** glow means the glimmerwort is healthy;
- a **red** glow means it is starving for energy;
- a **flickering** glow means it is budding.

## Root-mat sharing

When a glimmerwort sees a neighbor glowing red, it can grow its root-mat toward the neighbor until the two root-mats fuse. This is called root-pairing. Through the fused root-mats, the healthy glimmerwort passes glim to the starving one. Root-pairing only happens between glimmerworts that are within 2 meters of each other.
