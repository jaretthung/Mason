# Lesson 4 — Energy and Enzymes (slide deck, no speaker notes)

## Slide 1 — Energy in cells
- Energy = the ability to do work
- Metabolism = all chemical reactions in a cell

## Slide 2 — ATP
- ATP = adenosine triphosphate
- Adenine + ribose + 3 phosphate groups
- ATP → ADP + phosphate: releases energy
- ADP + phosphate → ATP: stores energy
- "Rechargeable battery"

## Slide 3 — Enzymes
- Enzymes = proteins that speed up chemical reactions
- Catalyst: not used up by the reaction
- Substrate = the molecule an enzyme acts on
- Substrate binds at the active site
- Enzymes lower the activation energy

## Slide 4 — Enzyme specificity
- Each enzyme fits a specific substrate
- Lock-and-key model
- Induced fit model

## Slide 5 — Factors that affect enzymes
- Temperature: optimum ~37 °C for most human enzymes
- pH: each enzyme has an optimum pH (pepsin in the stomach: ~2)
- Too hot or wrong pH → enzyme is denatured
- Rate rises with more substrate, then levels off

## Slide 6 — Enzyme inhibitors
- Competitive inhibitor: competes for the active site
- Noncompetitive inhibitor: binds somewhere else on the enzyme
